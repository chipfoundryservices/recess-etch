# Chapter 12 — Gate Replacement and Downstream Compatibility

## 12.1 The slit as a process pipe

After the etch, the slit becomes the conduit through which the stack is chemically rebuilt. Understanding those steps explains many of the etch's specifications.

## 12.2 Nitride removal

Hot phosphoric acid removes the sacrificial nitride selectively over oxide. The etchant moves from the slit into the interior of the block, laterally, through the nitride layer's own thin gap as it opens. Etch-front depth is limited by diffusion of the acid in, and of dissolved products out. Slit **profile** matters: a narrow or tapered slit gives a slower supply to the deepest layers. Uniformity of the lateral removal from top to bottom of the stack is a direct function of the slit's CD at every depth.

## 12.3 Collapse risk revisited

With nitride gone, the oxide layers are thin plates held only at the channel pillars. Cleaning and drying must not stress them (Chapter 8).

## 12.4 Word-line formation

The cavities are lined with a high-k blocking dielectric and a barrier, then filled with tungsten by a CVD/ALD process through the slit. This transport is again free-molecular or near-free-molecular inside the narrow gaps, so conformality needs a surface-reaction-limited regime. The slit's CD and sidewall condition determine whether the precursor reaches deep layers. Tungsten *also* deposits on the slit sidewalls, and must later be removed by an **etch-back**, so the slit must be wide and straight enough to allow the removal chemistry to enter.

## 12.5 Slit fill

Finally the slit is filled with dielectric, sometimes with a conductive core to form the source-line contact. Bowing, narrowing, or sidewall roughness can trap **voids** (seams) in the fill. A void is not necessarily fatal but may open during later CMP or etch steps.

## 12.6 Feedback to the etch team

Interface specifications from downstream teams usually include: minimum bottom CD, maximum top CD, sidewall angle, roughness limit, and residual-polymer limit. Each of these is a direct input to the etch recipe's targets.
