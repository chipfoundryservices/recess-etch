# Glossary

**Aspect ratio (AR)** — Feature depth divided by its width (slot) or diameter (hole).

**ARDE** — Aspect-ratio-dependent etching: etch rate that decreases with depth or aspect ratio.

**Bowing** — Outward bulge of a sidewall, usually just below the mask.

**Block** — The erase unit of a NAND array, bounded by slits.

**Channel hole** — Vertical hole through the stack that holds the memory-cell string.

**Charging (differential charging)** — Build-up of charge on insulating surfaces inside a feature that deflects incoming ions.

**Clausing factor** — Transmission probability of a tube in free-molecular flow.

**Cosine law** — Angular distribution, ∝ cos θ, of diffusely emitted or reflected molecules.

**Cryogenic etch** — Etching with the wafer cooled well below room temperature.

**Dummy channel** — Non-functional channel structure that gives mechanical support near a slit.

**Free-molecular (Knudsen) flow** — Gas flow in which molecules collide with walls far more often than with each other.

**Gate replacement** — Flow in which sacrificial nitride is removed and replaced with metal word lines.

**Hard mask** — Patterned film, often amorphous carbon, that withstands the etch.

**HAR** — High aspect ratio.

**Ion-neutral synergy** — Enhanced etching when ions and reactive neutrals act together.

**Knudsen number** — Ratio of molecular mean free path to feature size.

**Landing** — Stopping the etch on, or at a controlled depth into, the target layer.

**Monte Carlo (MC)** — Simulation by random sampling of particle histories.

**Notching** — Lateral etch at the bottom of a feature, at an insulator interface.

**Pulsed plasma** — Plasma whose power is modulated in time.

**Scalloping** — Periodic sidewall ripples, e.g. from alternating materials or cyclic processing.

**Sheath** — Boundary layer between plasma and surface in which ions are accelerated.

**Slit** — Long narrow trench through the whole stack, providing access for gate replacement and separating blocks.

**Source line** — Conductive layer at the bottom of the array contacted through the slit.

**Sticking coefficient (s)** — Probability that a molecule is lost on a wall collision.

**Tier** — One oxide–nitride pair of the stack.

**Tilt** — Departure of the feature axis from vertical.

**Transmission probability (W)** — Fraction of molecules entering a feature that reach its bottom.

**Wiggling** — Lateral oscillation of a slit along its length.
