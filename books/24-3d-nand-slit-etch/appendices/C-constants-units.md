# Appendix C — Constants, Units and Useful Conversions

## C.1 Constants

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Boltzmann constant k_B | 1.380649 × 10⁻²³ J/K = 8.617 × 10⁻⁵ eV/K |
| Elementary charge e | 1.602177 × 10⁻¹⁹ C |
| Electron-volt | 1.602177 × 10⁻¹⁹ J |
| Room temperature 300 K | k_BT ≈ 0.0259 eV |
| Atomic mass unit | 1.66054 × 10⁻²⁷ kg |

## C.2 Pressure

1 Torr = 133.322 Pa; 1 mTorr = 0.1333 Pa.

## C.3 Gas kinetics

Number density: n = P / (k_B T). At 10 mTorr and 300 K, n ≈ 3.2 × 10²⁰ m⁻³.

Mean thermal speed: v̄ = √(8 k_B T / π m). For a fluorine atom (19 u) at 300 K, v̄ ≈ 5.8 × 10² m/s.

Flux onto a surface: Γ = ¼ n v̄.

Mean free path (hard sphere): λ = 1 / (√2 π d² n). For d ≈ 0.4 nm at 10 mTorr (n ≈ 3.2 × 10²⁰ m⁻³), λ ≈ 0.4 cm, four orders of magnitude larger than a 100 nm feature, which is why transport inside the feature is free-molecular.

## C.4 Quick checks

- Aspect ratio AR = depth / width (slot) or depth / diameter (hole).
- Sidewall half-angle subtended by a slot of aspect ratio AR: arctan(1/AR); for AR = 60 this is about 0.95°.
- Lateral displacement of the slit bottom for a tilt α over depth d: d·tan α; for 1° over 6 µm this is about 0.1 µm.

## C.5 Layer count and depth

Depth ≈ (number of tiers) × (oxide + nitride thickness). With 60 nm per tier: 100 tiers ≈ 6 µm; 200 tiers ≈ 12 µm.
