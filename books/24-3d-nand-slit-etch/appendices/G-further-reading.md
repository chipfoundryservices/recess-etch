# Appendix G — Further Reading

The following are foundational references the book builds on. Citation details are given as the author remembers them; verify against the original before citing formally.

- M. Knudsen, "Die Gesetze der Molekularströmung und der inneren Reibungsströmung der Gase durch Röhren," *Annalen der Physik* 28, 75 (1909). Origin of free-molecular flow in tubes.
- P. Clausing, "Über die Strömung sehr verdünnter Gase durch Röhren von beliebiger Länge," *Annalen der Physik* 12, 961 (1932). Transmission probability of tubes.
- J. W. Coburn and H. F. Winters, "Ion- and electron-assisted gas-surface chemistry — an important effect in plasma etching," *Journal of Applied Physics* 50, 3189 (1979).
- R. A. Gottscho, C. W. Jurgensen and D. J. Vitkavage, "Microscopic uniformity in plasma etching," *Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology B* 10, 2133 (1992). ARDE mechanisms.
- G. S. Hwang and K. P. Giapis, "On the origin of the notching effect during etching in uniform high density plasmas," *Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology B* 15, 70 (1997).
- H. Tanaka et al., "Bit Cost Scalable Technology with Punch and Plug Process for Ultra High Density Flash Memory," *Symposium on VLSI Technology* (2007). BiCS.
- M. A. Lieberman and A. J. Lichtenberg, *Principles of Plasma Discharges and Materials Processing*, 2nd ed., Wiley (2005).
- D. B. Graves and others on feature-scale plasma–surface modelling; see the review literature in *Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology A* and *Journal of Physics D*.

*Note.* Process-specific claims in Chapters 5–16 describe widely discussed mechanisms in the field; they are not drawn from a single source, and numbers are illustrative (see Chapter 1).
